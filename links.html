Cool Links

These are some cool websites I have found and want to share with you.


  • Vimm's Lair - A website that has resources for playing older titles! It features pages dedicated to emulators, every ROM (out of 33 consoles and counting), ROM hacks, manuals, and even more links!

  • WiiLink (Previously WiiLink24) - This is a website for the Wii mod known as WiiLink. WiiLink allows the Wii to connect to the online services again, just like it used to. If anybody reading this is a developer of WiiLink I have an idea. Add the Wii Shop back and partner with Vimms Lair to download the WiiWare.

  • MeTube - This isn't really a website but its worth adding. MeTube is a fork of yt-dlp that adds a really nice WebUI. I use it a lot and I feel that you should too! I feel that for what it is people should self-host it more.
